milind-soni / OpenMausBot

+899 todayLaunched on X · 2026-08-11

Open-source Grok Bot alternative with a virtual machine that bots can use

The problem

Big AI companies launched ‘personal agent’ products that act for you on a cloud computer, but they are closed, locked to one company’s model, and require costly new subscriptions while keeping your data on their servers. People already paying for Claude or ChatGPT had no open, self-hosted way to get the same ‘team of AI helpers’.

OpenMausBot’s answer

OpenMausBot (formerly OpenGrokBot, also MausBot) is a free, Apache-2.0 desktop app for Mac, Windows and Ubuntu that looks like a messaging app. Each contact is an AI bot running on the user’s own Claude Code, Codex or Grok command-line tools, with its own personality, model, computer and connected apps, and risky actions need Allow/Deny approval.

ForDevelopers, solo founders and AI power users who already pay for Claude or ChatGPT/Codex and want a self-hosted, multi-agent alternative.
