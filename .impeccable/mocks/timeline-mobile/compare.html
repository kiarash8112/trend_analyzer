Making the mobile timeline read as time

Today every event gets the same space, so three events in one day look the same as a two-year silence. These directions use the real reports. Try macro to see a gap of more than two years, and OpenMausBot to see three events on one day.

Today

A list with a line behind it. Spacing follows text length, so it has no sense of time.

A. Measured line my pick

The vertical route. Real time sets the gaps, the line steps at launch, and the last 30 days are washed in blue.

Everything stays readable by scrolling. Long gaps are cut and labelled, so the scale is not strict.

B. Strip map

Your desktop route, kept horizontal and to scale and pinned to the top. Drag along it or tap Earlier and Later to read one event at a time. The round arrows at the bottom change repo, as they do on the site today.

The clearest overview, but reading every event takes many taps, and close events bunch up.

C. Month ledger

A timetable with one row per month, even the empty ones, and the year pinned as you scroll.

The easiest to scan by date, and the most rigid. It looks more like a calendar than a route.
